System Standards

Centrally Managed Platform Aesthetics

All global colors, themes, typography, and profile appearances are centrally configured by the platform administrator to ensure pristine performance, brand cohesion, and visual accessibility.

  • Locked Brand Harmony: Standardized Professional Blue palette with verified high-contrast readability across every section.
  • Read-Only Regular Accounts: Regular users enjoy a clean, distraction-free viewing experience without complex editing overhead.
  • Universal Device Calibration: Mobile-first layout engineered to load seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
🔒

Security & Governance Architecture

Administrator-Only Editing: Global theme colors, branding assets, content, and system settings require verified backend authentication and server-side role-based authorization (RBAC). No public or regular user editing controls are exposed in the frontend client.
